# GDG WOW Pune

## Women Techmakers & Google Developer Groups - Pune Chapter

Welcome to the official repository for GDG WOW (Women of Wonders) Pune! This repository hosts resources, event information, and community projects related to our GDG WOW initiatives in Pune.

### About GDG WOW Pune

GDG WOW Pune is a community-led initiative that aims to empower women in technology through mentorship, skill development, and networking opportunities. As part of Google Developer Groups and Women Techmakers, we organize events, workshops, and hackathons focused on emerging technologies and professional growth.

### Our Mission

- Foster an inclusive tech community in Pune
- Provide learning and development opportunities for women in tech
- Create a supportive network for knowledge sharing and collaboration
- Showcase women role models in the technology industry

### Events & Activities

- Technical workshops and hands-on sessions
- Panel discussions and speaker series
- Hackathons and coding challenges
- Networking meet-ups
- Mentorship programs

### How to Contribute

We welcome contributions from community members! Here's how you can get involved:

1. **Attend our events** - Check our social media channels for upcoming events
2. **Submit talk proposals** - Share your expertise by speaking at our events
3. **Volunteer** - Help us organize and run community activities
4. **Contribute to projects** - Submit PRs to our open-source projects
5. **Provide feedback** - Help us improve our community initiatives
